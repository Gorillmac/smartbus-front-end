ℹ️ How Tickets Work

SmartBus uses ticket numbers to verify passengers before they board. Here is what you need to know:

Step 1: Get your ticket number from the admin office or from your booking confirmation.
Step 2: Enter the ticket number in the box on the left and click "Verify Ticket".
Step 3: If valid, you can board the bus and use all passenger features.
Note: Ticket numbers are case-insensitive. Example: SB001, SB002

Sample ticket numbers to try: SB001, SB002, SB010
